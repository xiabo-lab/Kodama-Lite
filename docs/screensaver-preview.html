Kodama-Lite screen saver: sound-wave preview

Shown at the panel's 1920×440 shape. This page runs the same renderer and audio analysis code the app will use.

bands
  • Play demo beat plays a generated drum and bass loop. It has a quiet breakdown in the middle so you can see the wave settle down and then hit again.
  • Load a song uses one of your own audio files. It is decoded and analyzed the same way the app analyzes the playing track.
  • Pause shows what the screen saver looks like when nothing is playing: a still, straight line.
  • Full screen shows it the way it will look on the panel. Tap or press any key to exit.
